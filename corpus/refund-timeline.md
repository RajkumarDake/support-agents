# How long a refund takes
Once approved, the refund leaves us the same day. Card networks then take 5-7 business days, and
some banks take up to 10. SEPA direct debit reversals take 3-5 business days.

You get an email with the refund confirmation and a credit note when the refund is issued, not
when it lands in the account.
