# Adding a team member
Go to Settings > Team > Invite member, enter the email address, choose a role, and send. The
invitation email is valid for 7 days and can be resent from the same screen.

Only Owners and Admins can invite. A seat is consumed and billed only once the invitation is
accepted, prorated for the rest of the billing period. Starter workspaces are capped at 3
members; Pro at 25; Business and Enterprise are unlimited.
