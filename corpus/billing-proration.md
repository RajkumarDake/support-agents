# How proration works on plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated: you pay the difference between the plans for
the remaining days of the current period. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current
period, so you keep the higher plan's features until then and are never refunded the difference.

The prorated line item appears on your next invoice as `Plan change adjustment`. It is a
separate line, not a second charge.
