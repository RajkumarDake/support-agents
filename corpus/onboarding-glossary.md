# Glossary
Workspace: the top-level container that holds billing, members and projects.
Project: a scoped area with its own membership.
Seat: a billed, active member.
Period: the current billing month or year, which usage counts against.
Audit log: the immutable record of admin actions, exportable by Admins on Business and above.
