# Supported payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express, plus SEPA direct debit for EU customers on
annual plans. ACH and wire transfer are available on Enterprise contracts over $12,000 per year.

We do not accept PayPal, prepaid cards, or cryptocurrency. Only one payment method can be
primary; add a backup card so a single decline does not interrupt service.
