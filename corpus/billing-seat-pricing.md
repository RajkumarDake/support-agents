# How seats are counted and billed
You are billed per active seat. A seat becomes active the moment an invitation is accepted, and
is charged prorated for the rest of the period. Removing a member frees the seat immediately but
the credit appears on the following invoice.

Pending invitations do not consume a seat. Viewers on the Business plan are free and do not
count toward your seat total.
