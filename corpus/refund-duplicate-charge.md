# Refunds for duplicate charges
A duplicate charge is refunded in full and is not subject to the 14-day window. Support needs
both invoice IDs to confirm that two payments settled rather than one payment plus a pending
authorisation.

Pending authorisations that never settled are released by your bank in 3-5 business days and
there is nothing for us to refund in that case.
