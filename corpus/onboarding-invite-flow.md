# What your teammates see
An invited teammate gets an email with a single sign-up link. If their email domain matches a
verified domain on the workspace they skip straight in; otherwise they set a password or use
SSO.

New members land on a three-step tour and see only the projects they have been added to. Add
them to projects before inviting if you want their first screen to be useful.
