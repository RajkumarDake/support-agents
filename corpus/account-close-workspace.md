# Closing or deleting a workspace
Settings > General > Delete workspace, typed confirmation required, Owner only. Deletion is
scheduled 14 days out and can be cancelled by signing in during that window.

Export your data first: after the 14 days, backups are purged within 30 days and nothing can be
recovered. Deleting a workspace cancels the subscription but does not refund the current period.
