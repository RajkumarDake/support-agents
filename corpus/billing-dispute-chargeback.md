# Chargebacks and disputes
Please contact support before filing a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback automatically
suspends the workspace until the dispute closes, which can take up to 90 days, and we cannot
lift the suspension while the bank holds the case open.

Almost every dispute we see is a duplicate charge or an unrecognised company name on the
statement. Both are resolved by support in a few days.
