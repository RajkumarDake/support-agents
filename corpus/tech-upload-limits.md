# File size and type limits
Per-file limits are 100 MB on Starter, 2 GB on Pro and Business, and 10 GB on Enterprise.
Total workspace storage is 10 GB, 250 GB, 1 TB and custom respectively.

Allowed types: images, video, audio, PDF, Office documents, CSV, JSON, plain text and single
level zip archives. Uploads over 500 MB automatically switch to a chunked, resumable transfer.
