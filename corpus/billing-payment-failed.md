# Failed payments and card declines
When a charge fails we retry on day 1, day 3 and day 7. Your workspace stays fully active for
the whole 14-day grace period. On day 15 the workspace moves to read-only: your data is safe,
but you cannot write until a payment succeeds.

The most common decline reasons are an expired card, insufficient funds, and a bank block on
recurring international payments. Update the card under Billing > Payment method and the
outstanding invoice is retried immediately.
