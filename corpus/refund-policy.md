# Refund policy
We refund in full within 14 days of a charge if the workspace has not been used meaningfully in
that period. Duplicate charges and charges after a confirmed cancellation are always refunded in
full, with no time limit.

Annual plans cancelled mid-term are not refunded pro rata by default; the plan runs to the end
of the term. Exceptions for prolonged outages are decided case by case by a support lead.
