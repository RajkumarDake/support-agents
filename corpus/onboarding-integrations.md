# Setting up integrations
Slack, GitHub, Jira, Zendesk and Google Workspace connect from Settings > Integrations with
OAuth. Each integration asks only for the scopes it needs and can be revoked from the same
screen.

An integration is owned by the workspace, not by the person who connected it, so it keeps
working after that person leaves.
