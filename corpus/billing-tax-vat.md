# Tax, VAT and business details
Add your VAT or GST number under Billing > Business details. Once a valid number is on file,
future invoices are issued without tax where reverse charge applies. We cannot reissue past
invoices with a VAT number added retroactively, so add it before your next renewal.

The company name and address on the invoice come from the same screen and can be changed at any
time.
