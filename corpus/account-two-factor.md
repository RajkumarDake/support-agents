# Two-factor authentication
Enable 2FA under Settings > Security. We support authenticator apps (TOTP) and hardware
security keys. SMS is not supported.

Store the ten recovery codes somewhere safe: without a code and without the device, support
cannot restore access to an account with 2FA enabled, because we cannot verify identity any
other way. Admins can require 2FA for the whole workspace on Business and Enterprise.
