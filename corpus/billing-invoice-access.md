# Finding and downloading invoices
All invoices live under Billing > Invoices. Every invoice has a PDF download and shows the
period it covers, the amount, the payment method used, and its status: `paid`, `open`,
`failed`, or `refunded`.

Only workspace Owners and Admins can see the invoice list. Members and Viewers cannot.
Invoices are retained for seven years and can be re-sent to your billing email at any time.
