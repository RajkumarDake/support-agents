# Cancelling a subscription
Settings > Plan > Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
period; you keep full access until then and are not charged again.

If you were charged after cancelling, the charge is refunded in full - send us the invoice ID.
Cancelling does not delete your data; the workspace becomes read-only at the end of the period.
