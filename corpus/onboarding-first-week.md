# Your first week
Day 1: create the workspace, invite your team, connect your identity provider if you use one.
Day 2-3: import existing data with the CSV importer or the API.
Day 4-5: set up the integrations you actually use and turn off notifications you do not.

Most teams are productive after the import step. If an import stalls, it is nearly always a CSV
with inconsistent columns - the importer reports the offending row number.
