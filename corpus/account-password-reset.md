# Resetting a password
Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. The reset link is valid for one hour and can only be
used once. If SSO is enforced for your workspace, password reset is disabled and you must sign
in through your identity provider.

Resetting a password does not sign you out of other devices. Use Settings > Security > Sign out
everywhere for that.
