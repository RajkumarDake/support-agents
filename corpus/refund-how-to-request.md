# Requesting a refund
Contact support with the invoice ID and one line on what happened. A refund decision is made by
a human in one business day. We do not issue refunds automatically from the product, because
almost every request needs a judgement call about the plan and the usage.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5-7 business days to appear.
