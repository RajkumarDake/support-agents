# Duplicate or double charges
If you see two identical charges on the same day, it is almost always one of three things.

1. A retried payment. Our processor retries a card authorisation that timed out. The first
   authorisation drops off your statement within 3-5 business days on its own.
2. A real double charge. Two settled invoices with the same amount and the same date on your
   invoice list means both were captured and one must be refunded.
3. A plan change mid-cycle, which bills a prorated amount that can look like a second charge.

Check Billing > Invoices. If two invoices show status `paid` for the same amount and date,
contact support with both invoice IDs and we will refund the duplicate within 5-7 business days.
