# Roles and what each one can do
Owner: everything, including billing, deleting the workspace, and transferring ownership. There
is exactly one Owner.
Admin: manage members, integrations and settings, and view invoices. Cannot delete the
workspace.
Member: create and edit content in projects they belong to.
Viewer: read-only, free on Business and Enterprise.

Change a role under Settings > Team by clicking the role dropdown next to the person.
