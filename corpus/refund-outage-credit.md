# Service credits for outages
If a calendar month falls below 99.9% availability, Business and Enterprise customers can
request a service credit: 10% of the monthly fee under 99.9%, 25% under 99.0%, 50% under 95.0%.

Requests must be made within 30 days of the incident and reference the incident ID from the
status page. Credits are applied to the next invoice.
