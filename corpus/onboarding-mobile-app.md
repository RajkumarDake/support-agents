# Installing the mobile app
The app is on the App Store and Google Play. Sign in with the same credentials; SSO workspaces
open a browser window to the identity provider and return to the app.

The app is read-and-comment on Starter, and fully editable on Pro and above. Offline mode caches
the last 30 days of activity.
