# Removing a member
Settings > Team, then the overflow menu next to the person, then Remove. Their sessions end
within a minute and their API tokens are revoked immediately.

Content they created stays in the workspace and is reassigned to the Owner. The freed seat shows
as a credit on your next invoice. Removing someone is not reversible: re-adding them sends a new
invitation and starts a new seat.
