# Transferring workspace ownership
The current Owner opens Settings > Team, selects an existing Admin, and chooses Transfer
ownership. The Owner is downgraded to Admin in the same action.

The new Owner inherits billing responsibility but the payment method on file does not change.
If the original Owner has left the company and cannot sign in, support can transfer ownership
after verifying the domain with the billing contact.
